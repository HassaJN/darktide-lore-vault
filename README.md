# Warpglass: The Provenance Atlas for the Mourningstar

In the grim darkness of the 41st millennium, a warrior’s panoply is more than mere protection—it is a chronicle of suffering endured, heretics purged, and miracles barely survived. Yet the sacred cogitators aboard the *Mourningstar* have always been woefully taciturn, offering only a paltry glyph or a hushed whisper of a cosmetic’s origin. **Warpglass** shatters that silence. It is a forensic lens applied to your arsenal and wardrobe, peeling back the layers of codex data to reveal the full, bloody lineage of every item you carry. Where other tools merely list, Warpglass narrates—transforming a simple "Legionnaire’s Pauldron" into a saga of a specific week-long purge on Tertium, a particular cross-over incursion from a rival sector, or a challenge barely met with seconds to spare.

This is not a simple inventory dump. Warpglass is an auxiliary memory for the God-Emperor’s finest, a tool that ensures you never forget the *how* and *why* of your acquisitions. It bridges the gap between the silent armorium and your own legendary deeds, ensuring that every scarred chestplate tells its story in full, unflinching detail. Whether you are a veteran of the Moebian Sixth campaigns or a fresh-faced reject, this mod enriches your connection to your gear, turning passive collection into active remembrance.

## Overview: Beyond the Veil of Standard Data 🧠

The base game provides the "what" — a list of items. Warpglass provides the "why." It dives deep into the metaphysical threads of your inventory, offering a granular breakdown that feels less like a mod and more like an archaeological dig through your own history. It is designed for the collector who demands context, the lore-hound who seeks narrative, and the completionist who needs to know exactly which obscure condition was met to unlock that exclusive skin.

### Core Principles
- **Transparency:** Every asset has a story. Warpglass ensures that story is read aloud, not lost in the static.
- **Precision:** No vague descriptions. We deliver specific challenge names, completion criteria, and event designations.
- **Immersive UI:** The interface is designed to feel like a blessed cogitator terminal, merging seamlessly with the game’s existing aesthetic to avoid breaking your immersion in the grimdark world.

## The Provenance Decoder: Key Features 🔍

Warpglass is packed with modules designed to make your review of gear as detailed as a Mechanicus inspection log.

- **Specific Challenge Archival:** learn not just *that* you completed a task, but the exact name of the contract, the mission modifier, and the date of its completion.
- **Cross-Over Event Traceability:** Identify which limited-time incursion (be it a seasonal festival or a collaboration with another warzone) granted you that unique weapon skin. The mod adds a historical tag to each event.
- **Unlock Condition Analytics:** See the precise structural requirements — e.g., "Complete the 'Heresy' difficulty mission without taking damage from a Pox Hound," rather than the vague "Complete a secret task."
- **Warband & Penance Integration:** Detailed logs of specifically which penance track rewarded the item, including the sub-objectives you smashed through to get it.
- **Non-Linear Search Interface:** Filter your collection by *source type* (Event, Challenge, Crafting, Reward) or by *chronological acquisition date*, turning your inventory into a time machine of your service.
- **Responsive Cogitator UI:** Whether you are on a widescreen monitor or a compact viewport, the interface adapts fluidly, ensuring readability without cluttering the sacred screen space.
- **Multilingual Support (Binharic Standard):** Available in Low Gothic (English), High Gothic (French/German), and the binary cant of the Mechanicus (Mandarin), allowing a broader brotherhood of Rejects to utilize its power.
- **24/7 Tech-Adept Support:** We maintain a standing army of moderators on our community hub, ready to assist with any anomaly detection or data corruption issues.

## Installation & Integration: The Ritual of Binding ⚙️

Acquiring Warpglass is a ritual of a few simple steps, requiring no intervention from a Tech-Priest. The mod is packaged as a standard archive that integrates directly with your game directory’s mod loader. We do not utilize complex command-line incantations or require manual placement of scripts. You obtain the archive from the link above, then simply drop the contents into the designated `mods` folder of your game installation. ### Primary Requirements
- A legitimate copy of Warhammer 40,000: Darktide.
- The standard mod framework loader, as recommended by the community.
- A stable connection to the Warp (internet) for the initial data synchronization.

## The Interface: A Glimpse into the Dataslate 🖥️

Navigating Warpglass feels like adjusting a finely-calibrated augur array. The main inventory screen remains untouched, but a new icon—the stylized eye of the Emperor—appears on any item card. Hovering over it expands a panel on the right side of the screen, filled with dense, organized text. This panel uses a deep amber on dark parchment color scheme to reduce eye strain during long sessions. The information is tiered by hierarchical importance:
1.  **Acquisition Method:** (Challenge/Event/Forge).
2.  **Specific Achievement:** The exact parameters met.
3.  **Temporal Date:** The mission date (Imperial Standard Calendar).
4.  **Source Tag:** Cross-referenced with the official patch notes.

This layout ensures that you can glean the essential provenance at a glance, or dive deep into the specifics via a collapsible "In-Depth Data" section.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) 📖

**Q: Will this get me banned?**

**A:** No. This mod operates strictly on the client-side, reading data already present in your local game files. It makes no active calls to the server that alter gameplay. However, we recommend checking the official community guidelines regarding UI mods for absolute peace of mind.

**Q: I have items I obtained "before" the mod was installed. Will they show data?**

**A:** Yes. While we can trace the specific event logic for items acquired post-installation, legacy items often show "Timestamp Unavailable" but will still display the challenge or event name if it is present in the current data tables. We are constantly updating the database to back-fill historical data, but this is a slow process due to the sheer size of the Imperium’s records.

**Q: Is this a standalone tool or does it require other mods?**

**A:** It requires the standard "Mod Loader" framework to boot. Aside from that universal prerequisite, it is entirely standalone and does not depend on other UI changes.
